Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Windweaver (formerly SKYFLIP Arcade; "we," "our," or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile game, available on iOS. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

1. Information We Collect

Windweaver does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address. We do not require account creation or login to play. If you choose to email us, we receive what you send (see Section 11).

The App stores game progress, preferences, and settings (such as coins, strawberries, unlocked skins, high scores, and your daily wind streak) locally on your device. Windweaver does not upload your local save data to our own servers, and we do not operate servers, databases, or cloud storage for user data. Other sections of this policy describe information handled by our partners: scores and achievement progress submitted to Apple Game Center when you are signed in (Section 5), advertising-related data handled by Unity Ads (Sections 2 and 3), and in-app purchases processed by Apple (Section 4).

2. Third-Party Advertising

We use Unity Ads to show advertisements in the App: a banner ad at the bottom of the screen during flights only (there is no banner on the main menu, the shop, the locker, the purchase screen or the wheels), an interstitial (full-screen) ad when you return to the menu after a flight, and one optional rewarded video that you may choose to watch after a spin of the strawberry exchange wheel in the shop to double that spin's coin prize once (the Daily Spin has no video and pays its prize once). The normal wheel prize is paid whether or not a video is available; doubling happens only after a video has been watched to the end. Continuing a flight after a crash never requires watching a video. After you buy or restore Remove Ads, the App stops showing banner and interstitial ads; the optional exchange-wheel video remains available if you choose it, and prizes are never doubled automatically. If an interstitial ad is already on screen at the moment Remove Ads is bought or restored, that ad finishes; no new interstitial is shown afterwards, an ad that was only loaded in the background is never shown, and the banner is hidden immediately. Remove Ads changes which ads are shown; Unity Ads still runs for the optional wheel video, and the data practices in this section continue to apply. Unity Ads may collect certain information automatically, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as advertising ID)
  • A user-level identifier used by Unity's SDK (Windweaver has no accounts and does not give Unity a user ID of its own)
  • IP address and general location (country/region level)
  • Device information such as make, model, operating system and version, language, screen size, and connection type
  • Usage data, such as ad impressions, clicks and completions, app launches, and other interactions with the App and its ads
  • Purchase history (Unity lists this among the data its SDK may collect)
  • SDK performance data, such as initialization speed and energy use
  • Information you submit if you report an ad from within the ad

Unity uses this information to show and measure ads, for analytics, and to operate and improve its SDK, and may link most of it to the identifiers above. This data is collected and processed by Unity Technologies in accordance with their privacy policy. You can review Unity's privacy policy at https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

3. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS devices, we request your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before initializing Unity Ads. This permission controls whether apps may access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). You may choose to allow or deny this request. Declining does not prevent you from playing: if you deny tracking, Unity Ads is told to serve only non-personalized ads, which may be less relevant to your interests; depending on availability, fewer ads or no ads may be shown, and flights, the free Continue and base wheel prizes work the same either way (only the optional Shop strawberry-wheel doubling depends on a rewarded video being available and watched to the end). You can change your tracking preference at any time in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking (the App's Settings > Privacy Choices screen opens this page for you). The App reads your choice each time it starts or becomes active and applies it to Unity Ads (changing the permission in iOS Settings may restart the App).

3a. Privacy Choices in the App

Settings > Privacy Choices in the App shows the current tracking permission and offers a Personalized Ads switch. When tracking is allowed and the switch is on, Unity Ads may use your ad activity and advertising identifier to choose ads for you; when the switch is off, or whenever tracking is not allowed, Unity Ads is told to serve non-personalized ads. The switch is stored only on your device and takes effect immediately. If you have made no choice in the App, Unity Ads may show its own consent request where local law requires one, and you can use the data-privacy icon shown in Unity ads to manage your data with Unity directly. Remove Ads is a purchase that hides banner and interstitial ads; it is not a privacy setting and does not change these choices.

4. In-App Purchases

Windweaver offers optional in-app purchases, including coin packs and an option to remove ads. All purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect, process, or store any payment information, credit card details, or billing data.

Purchases use Unity's In-App Purchasing SDK, which runs on Unity Gaming Services. When the App starts it, Unity may receive technical diagnostic information from the SDK (such as performance and error reports) and a randomly generated installation identifier. This is handled by Unity Technologies under its privacy policy (linked in Section 2).

Windweaver keeps a local record of limited purchase-state information, including the Remove Ads entitlement and a short list of transaction identifiers used to prevent duplicate grants. The locally stored record is not payment-card or billing information. You can restore the Remove Ads purchase on a new device with the Restore Purchases button.

5. Game Center

The App integrates with Apple Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. When you are signed in to Game Center, Windweaver submits your scores to Game Center leaderboards and reports your achievement progress and completion to Game Center. Game Center is an Apple service, and your use of it is subject to Apple's terms and privacy practices. Windweaver does not receive or store your Apple ID credentials or payment details; sign-in and identity are handled by Apple.

6. Data Storage

Your game data (progress, settings, currencies, and unlocks) is stored locally on your device. Windweaver does not upload your local save file to our own servers, and we do not operate servers, databases, or cloud storage for user data. When you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your scores and achievement progress are submitted to Game Center (Section 5); advertising-related data is handled by Unity Ads (Sections 2 and 3). If you uninstall the App, your local game data will be deleted.

7. Children's Privacy

Windweaver is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain rights under GDPR. Since we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, data subject requests should be directed to our third-party partners (Unity Ads or Apple). You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time with the Personalized Ads switch in the App's Settings > Privacy Choices, through your device's tracking settings, or through the data-privacy icon in Unity ads, and you can withdraw consent for ad tracking at any time in iOS Settings.

9. Your Rights Under CCPA (California Users)

If you are a California resident, the CCPA provides you with specific rights. We do not sell personal information. To opt out of personalized advertising, turn off Personalized Ads in the App's Settings > Privacy Choices, adjust your device's tracking settings, or deny the ATT prompt on iOS.

9a. Versions of the App

This policy describes the App from version 1.0 build 21 onward. Test builds 18 to 20 distributed through TestFlight also showed the banner ad on the shop and locker screens and offered a prize-doubling video on the Daily Spin; builds 15 to 17 showed the banner ad on the main menu, shop and locker screens instead of during flights, offered a rewarded video to continue a flight after a crash, and did not include the Privacy Choices screen; everything else in this policy applies to them as well.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: skyflip2026@gmail.com. If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message, and we use it only to respond to you.
